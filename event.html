Arts and Culture

Autumn Arts Festival

September 25, 5:00 p.m. | Quad Lawn

Celebrate the creativity of the Aura University community with an evening of student art, live music, local food, and new connections.

About the festival

The Autumn Arts Festival brings student artists, performers, and makers together on the Quad Lawn for a welcoming night of creativity. Browse original work, enjoy performances, and discover the many ways to participate in campus arts.

What to expect

  • Student galleries and hands-on art demonstrations
  • Live music and spoken-word performances
  • Food trucks, refreshments, and community tables
  • Opportunities to meet campus arts organizations

Schedule

  1. 5:00 p.m. Festival opens and gallery walk begins
  2. 6:00 p.m. Live performances on the main stage
  3. 7:15 p.m. Community art activity
  4. 8:00 p.m. Closing performance

Accessibility and participation

The Quad Lawn has accessible routes and seating areas. ASL interpretation and a low-sensory gathering space will be available. Contact the organizer before the event for accommodation requests or participation questions.
